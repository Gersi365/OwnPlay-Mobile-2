PHYSICAL QA ACCEPTANCE SCOPE — PENDING EXPLICIT QA APK AUTHORIZATION
Live / playback:
- verify real video rendering in Preview and fullscreen;
- verify passive Preview without transport controls;
- switch channels and confirm the Preview switches cleanly;
- activate the same previewed channel again and confirm fullscreen without a presentation-only playback restart;
- Back from fullscreen returns to the same Preview;
- verify no duplicate audio, no stale/black surface, and explicit stop/exit behavior;
- verify PiP retains video plus audio and restores a clean surface;
- verify background/foreground and supported rotation outside PiP without ownership conflicts.

Library / VOD:
- verify Continue Watching hierarchy and approved artwork density;
- verify Movie and Series episode details;
- verify Resume persists and restores progress;
- verify Play from Beginning starts at zero without prematurely destroying prior saved progress;
- verify fullscreen VOD uses the unified player and custom OwnPlay controls.

Downloads / offline:
- verify QUEUED/DOWNLOADING -> Pause -> PAUSED persistence -> Resume -> COMPLETED;
- verify integrity-verified complete files only can play offline;
- verify Retry after recoverable failure does not create duplicate rows;
- verify offline playback and background/foreground behavior;
- verify Remove outside fullscreen.

Provider / data:
- verify representative Xtream and M3U/M3U8 sources on real networks;
- verify source refresh preserves favorites, hidden state, local names/order/groups, progress, and valid downloads;
- verify no credentials appear in UI, logs, ordinary backup, screenshots, or debug evidence.

Visual / accessibility / performance:
- capture physical screenshots for Live, Live Preview, fullscreen Live, Library, media details, Settings, and fullscreen VOD/offline;
- compare each primary screen directly against the approved project references;
- verify touch targets, TalkBack traversal/roles, font scaling, and readable contrast on-device;
- observe responsiveness with representative and large catalogs, downloads, refresh, and playback; no source-CI performance claim is substituted for device evidence.

CRITICAL BLOCKERS
- black video, audio-only PiP, duplicate audio, multiple active players, stale video surface, Preview/fullscreen/PiP crashes, presentation-only same-channel restart, continued playback after explicit stop, or critical resume corruption;
- credentials exposure, personalization loss on refresh, database corruption, nondeterministic identity duplication, download-state corruption, incomplete/unverified offline playback, or paused downloads resuming without explicit action.
